# Driftpilot charter

## Purpose

Driftpilot is a web-development studio marketing site and proof of the studio's own performance, design, and conversion standards.

## Current product shape

- Static, form-driven marketing site deployed on Vercel.
- Primary conversion goal: qualified contact and automotive early-access leads.
- The site is also a reusable framework: theme, content, and fonts are designed as swap layers for future client work.

## Guardrails

- Preserve the static-first, minimal-client-JavaScript architecture unless a business need proves otherwise.
- Keep the automotive funnel isolated from the general contact funnel.
- Do not publish unverified claims, pricing, or client proof.
